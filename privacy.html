TaiwanHaul Privacy Notice

Last updated: 2026-10-29

TaiwanHaul is operated by Josh Wang. This notice explains how TaiwanHaul handles information when you use the website.

Contact messages

If you contact us through the form, we collect your name, email address, Market, topic, and message so we can receive and respond to your inquiry. The form sends this information to our Cloudflare Worker, which delivers it by email using Cloudflare Email Routing. Contact messages are kept for 12 months after the inquiry is resolved, then deleted.

Bot protection

The contact form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to help detect and block automated abuse. Turnstile processes signals including your IP address, TLS fingerprint, and browser User-Agent. Read Cloudflare’s Turnstile Privacy Addendum.

Website request logs

Cloudflare Workers request logging is enabled. Cloudflare logs may include request and response details, method, URL, related metadata, errors, and Worker messages. Logs follow Cloudflare’s default retention for the account plan: 3 days on Free and 7 days on Paid.

Browser preferences

TaiwanHaul saves your Locale and Market choices in your browser’s localStorage so it can remember them when you return. These preferences use the keys th:locale and th:market; you can clear them in your browser’s site-data settings. The site code does not set cookies for these preferences.

Search and analytics

Search runs in your browser. TaiwanHaul does not send your typed search query to TaiwanHaul’s servers. The site’s analytics code creates coarse events and dispatches them locally in the browser; it does not transmit them to an analytics service.

Your choices and rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by emailing contact@taiwanhaul.com.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice when our data practices change. The latest version and its update date will appear on this page.
