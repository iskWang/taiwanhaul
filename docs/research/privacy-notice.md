# TaiwanHaul privacy notice research

## 1. Disclaimer

This is research for product planning, not legal advice. It summarizes selected primary legal texts and regulator/vendor guidance available as of 2026-10-28. Applicability depends on facts not established here; have qualified counsel confirm the final notice and legal obligations before launch.

The Worker validates the form, sends its contents as an email using its `SEND_EMAIL` binding, and passes the request IP to Turnstile verification. Sources: [`public/index.html:204-245`](../../public/index.html), [`public/js/contact.js:17-28,54-61`](../../public/js/contact.js), [`public/js/ui/contact-dialog.js:164-188`](../../public/js/ui/contact-dialog.js), [`worker/handle-contact.js:29-40,92-99,111-129`](../../worker/handle-contact.js). Cloudflare says invocation logs include the request, response, and related metadata; Fetch invocation entries show method and URL. Logs may also include custom `console` output and errors. Do not claim that it is or is not logged without checking the account's actual logs/configuration. **Inference:** Workers Logs may include request URL, method, status/response metadata, Cloudflare request context and errors; exact field set for this deployment is not established by the repo.

## 3. Privacy notice requirements by jurisdiction

Common practical elements reflected in the laws/guidance: who is collecting/controlling data; data categories and sources; specific purposes; whether provision is required and consequences of not providing; recipients/processors or disclosure; retention (where required or needed to explain use); rights and how to exercise them; contact details; and cross-border processing where applicable. Requirements differ; this table is a planning summary, not an equivalence claim.

| Jurisdiction | Primary-source disclosure baseline | Practical elements for TaiwanHaul |
|---|---|---|
| **Singapore — PDPA notification obligation** | PDPA s.20 requires informing the individual of the purposes for collection, use and disclosure on or before collection; disclose a new purpose before that use/disclosure. PDPC says purposes should be stated clearly enough for the person to understand, and recipients should be made known when relevant. [PDPC Advisory Guidelines, Ch. 14, §§14.1, 14.7, 14.15–14.18](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/-/media/files/pdpc/pdf-files/advisory-guidelines/the-notification-obligation---ch-14-(270717).pdf); [PDPC obligation overview](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/data-protection-obligations). | Explain why the form data is collected, email delivery, anti-bot processing and planned Unmet Demand use; do not use an open-ended “any business purpose” statement. A clear accessible online notice before submission is suitable guidance practice. |
| **Malaysia — PDPA 2010, Notice and Choice Principle** | Act s.7 requires written notice, in Bahasa Malaysia and English, describing that personal data is being processed and its purposes; source; access/correction and contact details; classes of third parties; choices/means to limit processing; whether supply is obligatory and consequences of not supplying; and rights to request access/correction. Notice is to be given as soon as practicable, before processing or when data is first collected. [Personal Data Protection Act 2010, s.7 (official commissioner portal)](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/en/akta/); [Commissioner's Guidance on Preparation of Personal Data Protection Notices](https://www.pdp.gov.my/ppdpv1/en/akta/guidance-on-the-preparation-of-personal-data-protection-notices/). | Include all listed items, including sources, required/optional fields and consequences, processor/recipient categories, rights, and a real contact channel. Provide Bahasa Malaysia and English if this Act applies; English alone is not a safe final position. |
| **Thailand — PDPA B.E. 2562, s.23** | Before or at collection, controller must notify purpose (including lawful basis), whether provision is legally/contractually required or needed to enter a contract and consequences of not providing, categories of personal data, retention period, recipient categories, controller contact details, DPO contact (if any), and data-subject rights. [PDPA B.E. 2562 (2019), official PDPC source](https://www.pdpc.or.th/en/10455/) (s.23); [English translation in the official Ministry of Digital Economy and Society law listing](https://www.mdes.go.th/law/detail/1905-Personal-Data-Protection-Act--B-E--2562--2019-). | State the controller identity/contact, data and purpose, disclosure recipients, required/optional status and consequences, rights/contact route, and retention periods once actually decided. Include DPO details only if one exists. |
| **Vietnam — law in force on 2026-10-28** | Law No. 91/2025/QH15 took effect 2026-01-01, and Decree No. 356/2025/ND-CP also took effect 2026-01-01. Under Law art.9, where consent is relied on, it is valid only when the person clearly knows the type of personal data, processing purpose, controller/controller-processor identity, and the data subject's rights and obligations. The law defines personal data broadly as information that identifies or helps identify a specific person (art.2). These are consent conditions, not a complete statement of any separate privacy-notice format. The official portal records the instruments but does not expose their full text in English; verify the Vietnamese text and implementing rules with counsel before finalizing all notice elements. [Law 91/2025/QH15 (Government legal-document portal; effective date shown in record)](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?classid=1&docid=214590&pageid=27160); [Decree 356/2025/ND-CP (Government legal-document portal)](https://vanban.chinhphu.vn/?classid=1&docid=216387&pageid=27160). **Article 9 summary cross-checked against an English translation; the translation is not primary-source authority.** | Identify controller, data types, purposes, rights, recipients/processors and retention in the notice as prudent practice; confirm exact statutory and decree requirements in authoritative Vietnamese before launch. |
| **Philippines — RA 10173 and NPC transparency guidance** | RA 10173 s.16 provides data-subject rights including being informed, access, correction, erasure/blocking and complaint, subject to law. The Act's transparency principle requires the data subject to be aware of the nature, purpose and extent of processing. NPC Circular 2020-03 §5 (data sharing cases) specifies categories of recipients, purpose/objectives, data categories, rights, and other information sufficient to understand nature/extent and manner of sharing; its application is specifically data sharing, not every controller notice. [RA 10173 (NPC official text), §§11,16](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act/); [NPC Circular 2020-03, §§3,5](https://privacy.gov.ph/wp-content/uploads/2021/01/Circular-Data-Sharing-Agreement-amending-16-02-21-Dec-2020-clean-copy-FINAL-LYA-and-JDN-signed-minor-edit.pdf). | Tell Visitors who controls data, what is processed, purpose and extent, recipients/processors and rights/complaint path. Identify only real recipients and review international processor locations before describing them. |
| **Taiwan — Personal Data Protection Act art.8** | When collecting personal data directly, the collector must inform the person of the collector's name; purposes; categories of personal data; time period, area, recipients and manner of use; and the person's rights and methods to exercise them. Exceptions appear in art.8(2). [PDPA art.8, Taiwan PDPC official English text](https://www.pdpc.gov.tw/en/News_Content/165/804/). | Include controller name, purposes, data types, retention, area, recipient and use method, plus rights and how to use them. Set actual retention and data flow before finalizing; do not guess. |

## 4. Findings for B–E

### B. Raw Query without IP

- **No-IP does not settle whether raw Query is personal data.** A Query can contain a name, email, phone number or other personal details; a single free-text field can also reveal sensitive/contextual information. Whether a particular Query is personal data depends on the applicable legal definition and whether a person is identifiable, including by combination with other information. **Inference**, not a direct regulator ruling on this site's search design. Philippine RA 10173 defines personal information by identifiability alone or in combination with other information (§3(g)); Taiwan PDPA art.2(1) covers information that directly or indirectly identifies a natural person. [Philippines RA 10173](https://privacy.gov.ph/data-privacy-act/); [Taiwan PDPA, art.2 (Ministry of Justice Laws & Regulations Database)](https://law.moj.gov.tw/ENG/LawClass/LawAll.aspx?pcode=I0050021).
- **Disclose planned Unmet Demand explicitly** as raw Query, Locale, Market and timestamp stored only after a zero-result search, for product planning. Say no account/IP identifier is intentionally stored with it only if implementation enforces this. Do not describe the feature as active until it ships.
- **Mitigations (recommended, not statutory quotations):** collect only the fields actually needed; warn Visitors not to put personal or sensitive information in Queries; scrub email addresses and phone numbers before storage; impose a short retention cap and then delete; limit access; aggregate counts/trends and discard raw Queries as soon as analysis permits. Scrubbing is imperfect, so do not promise that it removes all personal data. Keep request/log metadata separate and check whether keys, URLs or error logs can associate a Query with a Visitor. The specific mitigations here are recommendations/inferences, not requirements found in regulator guidance for search fields.

### C. Cloudflare Turnstile

Cloudflare's Turnstile Privacy Addendum says it processes client IP address, TLS fingerprint, User-Agent, sitekey and associated origin. It uses them to detect/block bots; Cloudflare says it cannot directly identify individuals from those Signals, including IP addresses. Cloudflare is the site's processor for website-security use and a controller for its separate improvement of Turnstile's bot detection. [Turnstile Privacy Addendum §§3–5](https://www.cloudflare.com/turnstile-privacy-policy/). Disclose the widget, signal categories/purpose, Cloudflare as recipient/service provider, and link the addendum. The repository uses explicit-render widget loading in the contact dialog (`public/js/ui/contact-dialog.js:19-29,64-70`). The addendum does not create a claim that Turnstile sets no cookies; avoid that assertion.

### D. localStorage consent

The code stores Locale and Market in `localStorage`, not cookies. I found no cited primary source establishing that consent is always required—or never required—for this exact preference storage in each of the six jurisdictions. **Inference:** because these values are preference settings and are not sent to an analytics vendor by current code, prior opt-in consent may not be required under the cited notice/consent rules; disclosure and transparency still make sense. This is not a jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction legal conclusion. Confirm local rules and whether platform/vendor behavior adds cookies or other tracking before launch. Malaysia's notice requires choice/means to limit processing (s.7); that is not the same as proof that a consent banner is mandatory for localStorage.

### E. Notice language

I found no one rule in the cited primary materials requiring every TaiwanHaul notice to be translated into every Visitor Locale. **Inference:** an intelligible notice in the audience's language is a strong transparency practice; an English-only notice is a weak fit for a site whose supported Locales are en, zh-TW, th, vi, ms and fil (`public/js/i18n.js:12-19`). Malaysia expressly requires Bahasa Malaysia and English under PDPA s.7(3) where applicable. Singapore PDPC recommends plain, comprehensible notices, but does not mandate a language list in the cited guidance. Translate the notice at least into supported Visitor languages as a product recommendation, subject to quality review.

## 5. Recommended notice outline

- Who runs TaiwanHaul and how to contact the responsible person.
- Contact form: fields, purpose, email delivery recipient/processor, required fields and what happens if not supplied.
- Cloudflare Turnstile: signals, anti-bot purpose, Cloudflare's role, and link to its addendum.
- Website request / Workers Logs: request and response metadata, purpose, Cloudflare storage, and plan-accurate retention; clarify whether bodies are logged only after checking actual configuration.
- Planned Unmet Demand: zero-result trigger, raw Query/Locale/Market/timestamp, no intentionally attached IP/account ID, product-planning use, safeguards and retention.
- Locale/Market preferences: localStorage purpose, keys/value types, and how to clear them.
- Recipients, international processing, retention for each data set, and deletion method.
- Visitor rights, request channel, complaint route where relevant, and how the notice changes.
- Effective date and supported translations.
