English

TaiwanHaul is a search and discovery site, not a shop. Product and price information comes from brands’ own official shops and may be wrong or out of date; check the brand’s site before buying. We plan to collect product information automatically once a day, but this collection is not running yet. TaiwanHaul is not affiliated with these brands.

Once collection begins, our bot will identify as TaiwanhaulDotComBot/1.0 (+https://taiwanhaul.com/terms). It will read at most 5 pages per shop per day, at least 5 seconds apart, and will obey robots.txt and Crawl-delay. A site can opt out by adding User-agent: TaiwanhaulDotComBot and Disallow: / to its robots.txt, or by emailing contact@taiwanhaul.com.
