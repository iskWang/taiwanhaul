English

TaiwanHaul is a search and discovery site, not a shop. Product and price information currently comes from merchant pages on e-commerce platforms and may be wrong or out of date; check the original site before buying. We plan to collect product information automatically once a day from brands’ official sites in the future, but this collection is not running yet. TaiwanHaul is not affiliated with these brands or platforms.

Once collection begins, our bot will identify as TaiwanhaulDotComBot/1.0 (+https://taiwanhaul.com/terms). It will read at most 5 pages per shop per day, at least 5 seconds apart, and will obey robots.txt and Crawl-delay. A site can opt out by adding User-agent: TaiwanhaulDotComBot and Disallow: / to its robots.txt, or by emailing contact@taiwanhaul.com.
